### Sql-Data-Analytics-Project

## Project Overview

This project analyzes **email marketing activity, user accounts, marketing costs, predicted revenue, and sales performance** using Google BigQuery.

The analysis combines data from multiple sources to evaluate email engagement, identify the most active countries, track marketing costs and predicted revenue over time, and compare revenue performance across continents and devices.

The final SQL queries provide datasets that can be used for further analysis, reporting, and dashboard creation.

## Objectives

* Analyze the number of accounts by country and user status.
* Calculate email marketing metrics such as sent, opened, and visited messages.
* Identify the top countries by number of accounts and sent emails.
* Analyze monthly email activity for individual accounts.
* Calculate the percentage of monthly emails sent by each account.
* Compare paid search costs with predicted revenue by month.
* Analyze revenue distribution across continents.
* Compare mobile and desktop revenue.
* Calculate the number of sessions and accounts by continent.
* Identify the number of verified accounts in each continent.

## Data Sources

The project uses the following tables from the `DA` dataset:

* `account` — account information and user statuses.
* `account_session` — connections between accounts and sessions.
* `session` — session dates and identifiers.
* `session_params` — geographical and session parameters.
* `email_sent` — sent email messages.
* `email_open` — email opening events.
* `email_visit` — visits generated from emails.
* `paid_search_cost` — paid search marketing costs.
* `revenue_predict` — predicted revenue.
* `order` — customer orders.
* `product` — product information and prices.

## Analysis Steps

### 1. Top Countries and Email Metrics

The first analysis combines account, session, and email data to calculate:

* number of unique accounts;
* number of sent emails;
* number of opened emails;
* number of email visits;
* account verification status;
* unsubscribe status;
* sending interval;
* country and date.

Window functions are used to calculate total account and email activity by country. Countries are then ranked based on the total number of accounts and sent emails.

The final dataset contains the **Top-10 countries by accounts or sent email messages**.

### 2. Monthly Email Activity

The second analysis examines email activity at the account level.

For each account and month, the following metrics are calculated:

* first email sending date;
* last email sending date;
* number of emails sent by the account;
* total number of emails sent during the month;
* percentage of all monthly emails generated by the account.

This makes it possible to identify accounts that contribute significantly to monthly email activity.

### 3. Marketing Costs and Predicted Revenue

The third analysis combines two sources:

* paid search costs;
* predicted revenue.

Data is aggregated by **year and month**, creating a unified dataset with:

* `year`;
* `month`;
* `cost`;
* `revenue`.

This dataset can be used to compare marketing spending with expected revenue and evaluate performance over time.

### 4. Revenue by Continent and Device

The final analysis evaluates sales performance by continent.

The following metrics are calculated:

* total revenue;
* revenue from mobile devices;
* revenue from desktop devices;
* percentage of total revenue;
* number of unique sessions;
* number of accounts;
* number of verified accounts.

This allows comparison of geographical performance and device contribution to total revenue.

## SQL Techniques Used

The project demonstrates the use of:

* `WITH` statements and CTEs;
* `JOIN` and `LEFT JOIN`;
* `UNION ALL`;
* aggregate functions such as `SUM()`, `COUNT()` and `COUNT(DISTINCT ...)`;
* conditional aggregation with `CASE WHEN`;
* window functions;
* `DENSE_RANK()`;
* `DATE_ADD()`;
* `DATE_TRUNC()`;
* `EXTRACT()`;
* percentage calculations;
* data aggregation by country, month, continent, and device.

## Key Outputs

The project produces several analytical datasets:

1. **Top countries by accounts and email activity**
2. **Monthly email activity by account**
3. **Monthly marketing costs and predicted revenue**
4. **Revenue, sessions, and account metrics by continent**

These datasets can be connected to **Looker Studio or other BI tools** for interactive reporting and visualization.

## Conclusion

The project provides a comprehensive view of **email marketing performance, user activity, marketing efficiency, and revenue distribution**.

By combining data from accounts, email events, sessions, marketing costs, and sales, the analysis helps identify the most important countries, evaluate email engagement, monitor marketing spending, and understand how revenue is distributed across geographical regions and devices.
